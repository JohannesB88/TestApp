Musical Symbols

Sharp (♯): Raises a note's pitch by one half-step.

Flat (♭): Lowers a note's pitch by one half-step.

Natural (♮): Cancels a previous sharp or flat.

What are Scales?

A scale is a sequence of notes arranged in a specific order of steps.

  • Major: Bright, happy, and common.
  • Natural Minor: Somber, serious, and moody.
  • Harmonic & Melodic Minor: Classical and jazzy variations.
How to Read Chords

A chord name has two parts: the Root and the Quality.

  • (none): Major (e.g., "C")
  • m: minor (e.g., "Cm")
  • dim: diminished (tense)
  • aug: augmented (unsettled)
  • 7: dominant 7th
  • sus: suspended (anticipatory)
  • add9: add 9 (rich, open)
